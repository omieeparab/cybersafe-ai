📖 Real-Life Cybersecurity Stories

Phishing Attack Example

Rahul received an email that looked like his bank, asking to reset his password. He clicked the link and lost access to his account. Lesson: Always verify the sender and never share OTPs.

Strong Password Success

Neha used a password manager to secure all her accounts. Even when one platform was hacked, her other accounts remained safe.

💡 Quick Tips

Use 2FA

Enable Two-Factor Authentication on all important accounts.

Check URLs

Always verify a URL before clicking. Hover and inspect the domain carefully.

Regular Updates

Keep your software and apps updated to patch vulnerabilities.
